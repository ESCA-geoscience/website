ProductTop 1,000 m

ESCA Subsurface: end to end, from log to model.

Geoscientists lose days moving data between a petrophysics package, a seismic package and a modelling package. ESCA Subsurface keeps the disciplines in one project, so a re-picked shale volume flows straight into porosity, pore pressure and the 3-D model.

Petrophysics

LAS import onto one regular depth index, shale volume, nine porosity methods and deterministic lithology from matrix lines.

  • Density
  • Neutron
  • Sonic
  • N-D crossplot
  • Gas correction

Stratigraphy & correlation

Zone sets, automatic marker correlation along each well path, interactive zone editing on the log, and cross sections with correlation lines.

  • TVDSS
  • Well groups
  • Fence diagrams

Seismic & interpretation

SEG-Y and ZGY linked without copying, 2-D lines and 3-D surveys, horizon and fault picking, the well–seismic tie and volume rendering.

  • SEG-Y
  • ZGY
  • Attributes
  • Well tie

3-D grids & geostatistics

Pillar grids, upscaling, variograms and trends, then property propagation by kriging or sequential Gaussian simulation, zone by zone and facies by facies.

  • Simple kriging
  • Ordinary kriging
  • Universal kriging
  • SGS

Pore pressure & mud window

Overburden, compaction trend, pore pressure by Eaton and Bowers, fracture and collapse gradients, and the safe mud window, drawn against the measured tests.

  • Eaton
  • Bowers
  • Matthews-Kelly
  • Daines

Machine learning

Train regression, classification and clustering models on your wells, then write the predicted log straight back to the project.

  • Regression
  • Facies classification
  • Clustering
WorkflowTop 2,150 m

One project, four steps, no exports in between.

Every result is written back into the same project and shows up in the same data tree, ready for the next discipline.

  1. Load

    LAS, deviation surveys, formation tops, core and pressure tests, SEG-Y and ZGY volumes, ZMap+ and CPS-3 grids.

  2. Interpret

    Compute petrophysical logs, paint facies, pick horizons and faults, and tie wells to seismic.

  3. Correlate

    Correlate markers between wells, check them on cross sections and maps, and grid the surfaces.

  4. Model

    Build the 3-D grid, upscale logs, fit variograms and propagate properties through the reservoir.

PrinciplesTop 2,870 m

Built the way geoscientists work.

Calculations checked against measurements

A computed curve is drawn beside the data that can prove it wrong: core plugs on porosity, pressure tests on pore pressure, leak-off tests on fracture gradient.

Your data stays readable

Projects are plain folders of text tables and JSON documents. Large seismic files are linked where they are, never duplicated.

Workflows you can re-run

Link several tools into one chain. Change the shale cutoff and every curve built on it updates on the next run.

Runs on your machine

A native Windows application that keeps working offline. Licences are per module, so you pay for the disciplines you use.
